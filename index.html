Dynamic Judge Network asks whether small, diverse evaluators can form a sparse reasoning path: each wave of evidence helps choose the next Judges, or tells the system to stop.

The High/Low benchmark and the network controller are research plans. The interactive model below is a hand-written illustration; it makes no Jev calls and reports no measured accuracy, latency, or savings.

RESEARCH QUESTION / 001THE NEXT JUDGMENT IS A DECISION

The idea

A network of judgments,
not a deeper single model.

DJN studies when additional judgment is useful, which specialist should run next, and when the evidence supports no action.

01

Facts first

Code computes exact features and checks data sufficiency before an uncertain Judge is asked anything.

02

Judges in waves

Independent Judges can share a request. Their answers form a compact Evidence State that guides the next wave.

03

Stopping is a choice

Counter-evidence may change a direction. Missing data, disagreement, or low actionability can produce SKIP.

07 / LOG

Decision trace

    Inspect the toy routing rules ＋

    These deliberately simple rules make the illustration reproducible. Their thresholds and weights are not calibrated to market data.

    1. Gate: completeness below 40% returns SKIP before any Judge question.
    2. Wave 0: Trend, Momentum, and Noise run together. Direction = 0.55 × Trend + 0.45 × Momentum; signed inputs are scaled to −1…+1. Disagreement = |Trend − Momentum| ÷ 2. Uncertainty = clip(1 − |direction| + 0.25 × Noise + 0.125 × disagreement).
    3. Route: another wave runs when uncertainty is at least 0.55, reversal risk at least 0.55, or disagreement at least 0.45. A one-wave budget returns SKIP when more evidence is needed.
    4. Wave 1: a Counter-HIGH or Counter-LOW Judge challenges the preliminary direction. Mean Reversion joins when reversal risk is at least 0.45 or noise at least 0.55. Counter strength = clip(0.5 × Reversal + 0.25 × Noise + 0.25 × disagreement); it reduces the preliminary score, with an additional reversal adjustment when Mean Reversion runs.
    5. Stop: the final direction is used only when its score magnitude is at least 0.34, uncertainty at most 0.68, and actionability at least 0.55; otherwise the result is SKIP. Actionability falls as noise, reversal risk, disagreement, missing data, or counter-evidence rises.

    Read the counts carefully

    A question asked is not always evidence used.

    01

    Requested

    A question was sent to the illustrative provider boundary. A failed call would still count here.

    02

    Fetched

    An answer returned. In this toy, every request succeeds; real experiments must log failures.

    03

    Activated

    The controller placed the Judge on the reasoning path. A prefetched answer may never be activated.

    04

    Used

    The evidence affects routing or the final decision. All activated Judges in this toy are used.
